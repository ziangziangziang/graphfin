## Initial generation

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: photorealistic concept preview for the GraphFin website's 3D hero sculpture; a design study, no webpage UI.
Primary request: a rounder open capital G built from a continuous graphene-style carbon lattice ribbon, retaining pronounced, believable camera depth. The upper-right free terminal reaches OUT TOWARD THE VIEWER; the inner central free terminal recedes AWAY FROM THE VIEWER behind the middle plane.
Scene/backdrop: nearly black charcoal #101315 studio background. One thin muted amber rectangular frame behind the sculpture establishes a screen/window plane. The G overlaps and projects beyond that frame slightly.
Subject geometry: one continuous OPEN G stroke, an almost circular outer loop with balanced height and width, a clear right aperture and a broad smooth lower-right inward bend leading to a short central horizontal arm. The upper-right terminal at approximately 2 o'clock is the nearest part of the sculpture: its ribbon bends forward in depth toward the lens, with visibly larger foreground atoms and bonds. The central inward terminal in the aperture points backward away from the lens, visibly smaller and partially occluded by the front limb. Both terminals have intentional physical edges, no disappearing or dissolving ends. Keep the overall G highly legible.
Materials: the ribbon consists of small graphite spheres joined by slender graphite rods into continuous hexagonal honeycomb cells, approximately three cells across, with coherent bonds and physically plausible spacing. Graphite has subtle grey-green reflections, fine surface grain, varied roughness and modest metallic highlights. A short connected section of actual bonds on the lower-left outer curve glows warm amber-gold as a traveling signal with restrained light spill. The remainder stays graphite.
Style/medium: premium photorealistic studio product photograph of a real freestanding lattice sculpture, tactile and optically convincing. No flat vector appearance.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 image, single large G centered with generous breathing room, full object and all tips visible. The nominal circular G is seen slightly obliquely while retaining its round silhouette.
Camera and depth: a genuine perspective camera with a roughly 45-50mm full-frame lens, modest front three-quarter angle. Strong but believable foreground/background scale differences and occlusion. Focus falls on the outer mid-band and the forward upper-right tip, with a naturally gradual and moderate depth-of-field falloff into the recessed central arm. Foreground detail is legible; background blur is optical, not a grey fade. Preserve visible perspective depth rather than flattening into an orthographic icon.
Lighting: broad warm studio key from upper left, subtle cool graphite-green rim, physically motivated specular reflections, soft local shading. The amber pulse spills onto adjacent bonds only.
Constraints: exactly one open G sculpture and its thin background frame; no central gold ring, no bridge joining the tips, no extra loops, no stacked letters, no opaque solid tube, no floating disconnected beads, no neon outline, no text, no annotations, no buttons, no watermark. Prioritize the upper-right near / central tip far depth reversal and a circular G silhouette.

Retain full outline and breathing room. The result should look like the same object photographed with the same lens and lighting, merely manufactured as a thinner open lattice ribbon.
